How it works

  1. Choose a folder. mediakeep scans it and works out what each movie or show is.
  2. Choose the destination. Rename everything where it is (the default), or move it into another folder.
  3. Check the list. Fix anything marked "Needs you", tick what you want and add it to Execute.
  4. Apply on Execute. Every change can be undone from History & undo.
